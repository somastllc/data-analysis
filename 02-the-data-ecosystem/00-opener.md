*On screen: the five terms appearing one per beat, then the six lesson titles.*

Analytics. Business intelligence. Data science. Machine learning. Data engineering.

Those five terms get used as though they were interchangeable. They are not, and the people who use them that way are usually the ones asking you for something impossible.

Chapter two is the map. Six lessons.

You will learn the four types of analytics - what happened, why it happened, what might happen next, and what to do about it - and why a question about the past takes completely different work from a question about the future.

Then each field in turn. What data analysis and business intelligence each do. Where data science and machine learning actually differ, which is not where most people think. And what data engineers build that everybody else quietly depends on.

The last lesson is the one that matters most. It takes a single event - one customer cancelling a subscription - and shows five different people touching it, not one of whom could have handled it alone.

You know what data is. This chapter is who handles it.
