# Data Analysis and Business Intelligence

**Chapter:** 2 — The Data Ecosystem  
**Lesson:** 2 of 6  
**By the end:** You can explain what data analysis and business intelligence each do, and where they overlap.

## Overview

A shop owner notices that one product sells out every week while another sits on the shelf. She wants to understand why — and she wants a simple way to check sales every month without digging through files each time. That is where **data analysis** and **business intelligence (BI)** come in. Both focus on understanding data to support decisions, especially in business settings.

### What is data analysis?

**Data analysis** is the process of inspecting, cleaning, transforming, and interpreting data to discover useful information and support conclusions.

A data analyst might:

- Explore sales trends
- Compare performance across regions
- Identify reasons for customer churn
- Prepare reports for managers

Data analysis often answers questions like:

- Which products sold the most?
- Why did revenue drop in March?
- Which customer group is most profitable?

### What is business intelligence?

**Business intelligence (BI)** focuses on turning data into dashboards, reports, and metrics that help organizations monitor performance and make decisions.

BI often emphasizes:

- Key performance indicators (KPIs)
- Interactive dashboards
- Regular reporting
- Accessible insights for business teams

Tools like Power BI and Tableau are commonly used in BI.

### How they relate

Data analysis and BI overlap significantly.

- **Data analysis** is the broader activity of working with data
- **BI** is often the business-facing side of that work, focused on reporting and monitoring

Think of BI as a practical application of data analysis for business users.

### Example

A retail company wants to track monthly sales.

- A **data analyst** explores the data and finds that one region is underperforming
- A **BI dashboard** shows daily sales, top products, and regional comparisons for managers

Both roles support the same goal: better business decisions.

## Key Terms

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Data analysis** | The process of examining data to find patterns and answer questions |
| **Business intelligence (BI)** | Tools and practices for reporting and monitoring business performance |
| **KPI** | A key performance indicator used to measure success |
