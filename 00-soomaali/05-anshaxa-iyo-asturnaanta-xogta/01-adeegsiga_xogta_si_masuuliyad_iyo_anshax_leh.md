# Adeegsiga Xogta si Masʼuuliyad iyo Anshax Leh

**Cutubka:** 5aad - Anshaxa iyo Asturnaanta Xogta  
**Casharka:** 1aad ee 4-ta cashar  
**Dhammaadka casharkan:** Waxaad awoodi doontaa inaad sharaxdo mabaadiʼda isticmaalka xogta si masuuliyad leh, isla markaana aad aqoonsato jaantus marin-habaabin ah, xitaa haddii tirooyinka ku jira ay sax yihiin.

## Dulmar

Mashruucyada xogta ee keena waxyeellada ugu weyn badanaa ma dhisaan dad leh ujeeddo xun. Inta badan waxaa dhisa kooxo niyad wanaagsan leh oo aan aad uga fiirsan in waxa ay samaynayaan ay habboon yihiin iyo in kale.

**Anshaxa xogta (data ethics)** waa adeegsiga mabaadiʼ naga caawinaya inaan xogta u isticmaalno si masuuliyad leh, caddaaladna ku jirto.

Afarta cashar ee cutubkan waxay daba socdaan hal mashruuc oo ku saabsan shirkad adeegga lacagaha moobilka bixisa oo samaynaysa adeeg amaah. Mashruuca waxaa laga eegi doonaa afar dhinac oo muhiim ah: adeegsiga xogta si masuuliyad leh, asturnaanta (privacy), eexda (bias), iyo maamulidda xogta (governance).

### Mashruuca Amaahda Ganacsiga

Shirkad adeegga lacagaha mobile-ka bixisa ayaa leh malaayiin macaamiil ah oo lacag dira kuna kaydsada telefoonadooda. Shirkaddu waxay goʼaansatay inay bilowdo adeeg amaah yar-yar oo ganacsi, kaas oo si otomaatig ah loogu ansixin karo daqiiqado gudahood.

Si loo goʼaamiyo cidda u qalanta amaahda, kooxdu waxay macaamil kasta siinaysaa dhibco qiimayn ah (score), iyadoo adeegsanaysa xog ay shirkaddu hore u haysay, sida:

- Lacagta akoonka soo marta bil kasta
- Muddada uu akoonku jiray
- Degmada lambarka telefoonku ka diiwaangashan yahay
- In macaamilku hore u helay lacag-bixin ganacsi iyo in kale

Dusha marka laga eego, mashruucu waa mid wanaagsan. Xogtu way jirtaa, fursadda ganacsiguna way xooggan tahay. Laakiin ku dhawaad dhibaato kasta oo anshaxeed oo cutubkan lagu baranayo waxay ku dhex qarsoon tahay mashruucan.

### Marka Tiradu Sax Tahay, Laakiin Chart-ku Marin Habaabiyo

Ka hor inta adeegga si buuxda loo bilaabin, kooxdu waxay samaysay tijaabo kooban, kadibna waxay soo bandhigtay heerka amaahda dib loo bixiyey (repayment rate):

| Bisha | Heerka dib-u-bixinta |
|---|---:|
| January | 91.0% |
| February | 91.4% |
| March | 91.2% |
| April | 92.0% |

Tirooyinkan waxaa loo sawiri karaa laba qaab oo kala duwan.

**Chart A: Axis-ku wuxuu ka bilaabmaa 90%, kuna dhammaadaa 92%**

```text
Jan  ##########
Feb  ##############
Mar  ############
Apr  ####################
```

Chart A wuxuu April u ekaysiinayaa inay aad uga sarreyso January. Sababtu waa in axis-ku ka bilaabmayo 90%, sidaas darteed farqiga yar ee u dhexeeya 91% iyo 92% ayaa muuqaal ahaan aad loo buunbuuniyey.

Chart A tiro been ah kuma jirto, laakiin waa **jaantus marin-habaabin ah (misleading visualization)**. Xogtu waa sax, balse qaabka loo soo bandhigay ayaa qofka siin kara faham khaldan.

### Calaamadaha Digniinta

Marka aad eegayso ama samaynayso chart, hubi arrimahan:

- Dhidibka (axis) oo aan eber ka bilaaban, iyadoo aan jirin sabab cad
- Farqi yar oo muuqaal ahaan loo buunbuuniyey
- Boqolley la soo bandhigay iyada oo aan la muujin tirada laga soo xisaabiyey, sida koror 50% ah oo ka yimid laba macaamiil una kordhay saddex

### Mabaadi’da Adeegsiga Xogta si Masuuliyad Leh

| Mabdaʼa | Macnaha |
|---|---|
| **Daahfurnaan (transparency)** | Si cad u sheeg xogta la ururinayo, sababta loo ururinayo, iyo sida loo adeegsanayo |
| **Ku koobnaanta ujeeddada (purpose limitation)** | Xogta u adeegso oo keliya ujeeddadii loo ururiyey |
| **Saxnaan (accuracy)** | Go’aannada ha ku salayn xog khaldan, aan dhammaystirnayn, ama wax marin habaabinaysa |
| **Caddaalad (fairness)** | Ka fogow waxyeellada qof ama koox |
| **Masʼuuliyad qaadasho (accountability)** | Qaado masʼuuliyadda sida xogta loo adeegsado |
