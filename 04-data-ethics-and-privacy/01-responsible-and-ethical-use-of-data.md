# Responsible and Ethical Use of Data

**Chapter:** 4 - Data Ethics and Privacy

**Lesson:** 1 of 4

**By the end:** You can explain responsible data use and name questions to ask before sharing results.

## Overview

Data can harm people even when the numbers are correct. The harm may come from how the data is collected, used, or presented.

### Data Ethics and Responsible Use

**Data ethics** means using data responsibly, honestly, and fairly.

| Principle | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Transparency** | Clearly explain what data is collected and how it is used |
| **Accuracy** | Use correct data and present it honestly |
| **Fairness** | Avoid unfair harm to a person or group |
| **Accountability** | Take responsibility for how data is used |

Before sharing results:

- Could this harm a person or group?
- Did we include important information?
- Is the result clear and honest?
- Could someone misunderstand what the result means?
