# Communicating Insights

**Chapter:** 3 — The Data Analysis Process  
**Lesson:** 5 of 6  
**By the end:** You can present the same finding at the right level of detail for different audiences.

## Overview

An analyst spends three weeks on an investigation, presents twelve slides, and nothing changes. The finding was correct. It was also the fourth thing mentioned, on slide nine. Finding an insight is only half the job. You must communicate it clearly so others can understand and act on it.

### Analysis without communication has limited value

A strong insight that nobody understands will not change anything.

Good communication helps you:

- Explain what you found
- Show why it matters
- Recommend next steps
- Build trust in your analysis

Data professionals must be clear thinkers and clear communicators.

### What to include in a data report

A simple report often includes:

1. **Context** — what problem you investigated
2. **Method** — what data you used
3. **Findings** — what the data showed
4. **Visuals** — charts that support the story
5. **Recommendation** — what action to consider

Keep the message focused. Most stakeholders do not need every detail of your process.

### Tips for clear communication

- Start with the main takeaway
- Use plain language, not jargon
- Highlight the most important numbers
- Choose visuals that support your message
- Explain limitations honestly

Example:

- Weak: "The regression output indicates a negative coefficient."
- Strong: "Customers who wait more than 3 days for support are twice as likely to cancel."

The second version is clearer and more actionable.

### Know your audience

Different audiences need different levels of detail.

- Executives may want a short summary and recommendation
- Analysts may want methodology and data details
- Product teams may want specific user behavior insights

Tailor your message to the person making the decision.

## Key Terms

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Insight** | A meaningful conclusion, supported by data, that helps guide a decision |
| **Recommendation** | A suggested action based on analysis |
