Data Governance and Regulations

By the end:

You can explain how data governance and regulations help an organization manage data.

Overview

Protecting data should not depend on someone noticing a problem by chance. Clear rules and responsibilities should be part of the work.

Data Governance

Data governance is the rules and roles an organization uses to manage data responsibly.

QuestionRule
Who is responsible?Assign a data owner
Who can see the data?Give access only to people who need it
Why is the data kept?Record its purpose
When should it be removed?Set a deletion date

These rules make responsibility clear before a problem occurs.

Regulations

A regulation is an official rule or law about how data is collected, stored, and used.

Data laws vary by place and by the type of data being used. As a beginner, you do not need to memorize every law.

In your work:

  • Follow your organization’s data policies
  • Ask before sharing sensitive data
  • Respect access restrictions
  • Report data that is being misused or poorly protected
