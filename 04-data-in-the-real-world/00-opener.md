So far you have not installed anything. This chapter is where you find out what you will install, and why.

Four lessons.

The first is the toolkit — spreadsheets, SQL, Python, dashboards, version control — and, more useful than the list, when to reach for each one. The rule you will take away is that the right tool is the smallest one that does the job, which is the opposite of what most beginners assume.

The second is why analysts write code at all, and it is where you see code for the first time in this course. A few lines of SQL, a few lines of Python. You are not expected to understand them. You are expected to see what the work looks like.

The third is careers. Six job titles, what each one is actually paid to answer, and how people move between them.

The fourth is industries: a hospital, an online store, and a school, hiring identical skills for completely different questions.
